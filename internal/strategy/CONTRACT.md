# Strategy policy contract

A policy artifact is a JSON object with `schema_version` (integer 1), nonempty `policy_version`, `rules_version`, `catalog_version` strings, `payload` (nonempty bytes encoded as standard base64), and `digest`. Payload semantics belong to the producer and policy implementation; envelope validation alone does not establish that a policy can execute the payload. Validate and construct the policy before selecting it for use.

The digest is `sha256:` followed by lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256. Hash these five byte strings in order: decimal ASCII schema version, UTF-8 policy version, UTF-8 rules version, UTF-8 catalog version, raw payload. Prefix each with its length as an unsigned 64-bit big-endian integer. The digest detects corruption; it does not authenticate the producer. `MarshalArtifact` writes the envelope and `LoadArtifact` checks schema, compatibility and digest. `Select` returns a reason on rejection and preserves the last selected artifact. Selecting an artifact does not promote it in an evaluation workflow.

Executable payload v1 is UTF-8 JSON `{"kind":"action-ranker-v1","card_weights":{"card-id":10},"kind_weights":{"play":1,"pass":0}}`. Both weight maps may be omitted, and values are signed 32-bit integers. A native candidate ID is the JSON encoding of `rules.Action`, including `Kind` and `Card`. The score is the kind weight plus the card weight for plays; missing weights are zero. The first candidate in baseline order wins ties. A malformed or unsupported action rejects the proposal, leaving the normal validated fallback available. This ranker is descriptive/configured and makes no claim of improved play.

Create `ExecutablePolicies` with a baseline version and baseline policy (nil means ordered fallback). `Select` validates the envelope and constructs the ranker before changing the selection; a rejected candidate returns its reason and keeps the previous policy. At a new match boundary call its `BeginMatch` and retain the resulting version and policy throughout the match. Use `NewVersioned(snapshot.Policy, snapshot.Version)` and set `Observation.PolicyVersion` to that version. A selection during a match affects later snapshots only. The legacy `New` binds the empty version for callers without artifact metadata.

An observation binds match, revision, deadline, state, ordered candidate IDs and policy version. The service copies input, rejects conflicts and stale results, and checks proposals against the original candidate set. The caller must validate current legality and freshness, serialize observation changes and execution, and reserve time before the deadline to call `ResolveFallback`. `Poll` does not wait for the policy. Policy implementations must honor context cancellation; validation and execution must be bounded by the caller. No network or remote policy is required.
